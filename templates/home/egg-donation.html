The Process

Four clear stages

01

Apply & screen

Health history, bloodwork, ultrasound and counselling.

02

Recipients are matched with a verified donor profile.

03

Stimulate

10–12 days of monitored hormone injections.

04

Retrieve

A 15–20 minute sedated procedure, home the same day.

Donor Eligibility

Could you be a donor?

  • Aged 21–32 with regular menstrual cycles
  • Non-smoker with a healthy BMI range
  • No untreated hereditary conditions in close family
  • Able to attend monitoring visits over two weeks
