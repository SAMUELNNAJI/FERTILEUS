Our Story

Why we started

We kept meeting far too many people facing fertility challenges alone — without access to the best specialists, reliable medical information and nobody willing to ask. We built FertilEus so that no one has to face this in the dark.

Today we reach across 15 African countries with more than 50 certified clinics, and a community of over 10,000 families who found answers here first.
